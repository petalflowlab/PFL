Evolution

Getting ready for a V2. that moves with you.

Built From Scratch. Ready to Be Built Right.

Every part of the Petal Staff was designed from the ground up — the petal heads, the electronics, the assembly. It has been reborn and torn apart and is ready for a more stable life in an LED space with you.

On its final path to looking good and feeling good, 3 major tweaks remain.

🌸 Silicone Molded Petals

The 3D printed petals have held up beautifully — smashes, drops, contact staff abuse, poi life — they've taken it all but larger dragon petals are a different story. For some spinners they've met their breaking point, which was always the risk with that size.

The good news? They're easily swapped out. But for V2 I'm looking to survive it all. Silicone molded petals will be necessary. More robust, more reliable, and built to keep up with the hard knock life of a flow prop.

📡 Motion-Sensitive LEDs

A custom PCB with a 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope tracks your exact speed, tilt, and rotation in real time. The LEDs respond to what you're actually doing — not a preset loop. Fast spin triggers a bloom. A slow roll closes it back down. It knows the difference.

Testing has revealed only minor issues but great durability on impacts. Layout needs a slight revision and with a complete rework of LED connection point was needed to keep the LEDS secure and wiggle free.

📏 3-Part Collapsible Design

A 3 piece design was needed. This helped with the motion tracking with a sensor on both ends, portability, and battery life/size.

Engineering Backbone

Currently features 32 special movement-reactive modes and fully customizable color palettes from bluetooth app. From martial arts combat sequences to fluid contact rolls, you can dial in the exact aesthetic and behavior to match your flow style.

A 3 piece design was needed. This helped with the motion tracking with a sensor on both ends, portability, and battery life/size. The 3-piece layout allows independent battery and sensor placement on each section — perfectly balanced, and with better battery life to match. It adds a little to the cost, but the payoff in accuracy and portability makes it a no-brainer.

Industry standard tough. The 3-piece clear and strong polycarbonate tubes with long overlaps avoids flexing problems. Hard drops are well protected.

More LEDs meant more power drain, so a full-size 18650 battery was chosen to add weight and provide enough power. Our target is at least 10 hours of flow on a charge — USB-C charging won't be needed til morning.

A built-in microphone syncs the LEDs to the music around you in real time. Beat drops trigger flashes, pulses, and reactive waves across the full LED strip. Dial the sensitivity through the app and let the room control your lights.

The Latest Evolution

Enter the Flow Sword.

1 Capsule. Two Options. Pure Momentum.

I've been working on a flow sword with the exact petals and capsule. The sword moves really differently from a staff so the effects are adjusted for this. The final design is a much simpler build that I can produce easily and for less, without sacrificing durability.

⚒️ Sturdy & Reliable Build

Designed for impact. The blade is a high-grade polycarbonate tube housing a double-sided LED strip for maximum visibility. The motion-tracking capsule sits safely within the polycarbonate hilt, making the entire prop stiff, sturdy, and extremely reliable.

It's a simple, elegant assembly that's built to take the abuse of contact sword play and high-speed strikes.

🌸 The Same Blooming Principle

The sword dynamically shifts its weight and profile based entirely on how you move it. Like with the staff, the petals give extra balance control for fishtails and light up beautifully.

They also provide a solid cushion for the sword to land on if you dare drop it! The LEDs are currently being custom fine-tuned specifically for these exact movement dynamics.

The Little Details.

Transparency is key for a project like this. These are the engineering decisions, trade-offs, and "work-in-progress" items you should know about.

🔋 Battery Size & Life

The final battery size isn't set in stone. Current builds last over 12 hours. Moving to a two-battery design allows for smaller individual cells while keeping life identical since we're powering fewer LEDs per board.

🛡️ Staff Rigidity

Polycarbonate tubes have some natural flex. We use extremely long overlaps to keep it minimal. If you want maximum stiffness, the middle section can be swapped for Carbon Fiber (though you'll lose a few LEDs on the inner ends).

⚡ Robust Electronics

The boards don't break. We've redesigned the LED attachment point to be reinforced directly to the PCB, fixing the one weak link from V1. We use high-quality LED strips instead of custom-manufactured boards because they are more drop-resistant – a preferred choice for jugglers.

🎨 Mode Readiness

Are the modes finished? No. While the code is functional, it needs significant optimization and sensor calibration to look correct. This will be my primary focus from now until shipping.
